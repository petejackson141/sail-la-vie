Delete your account

NimoSail, by Pete Jackson · Last updated 2 November 2026
In short: you can delete your NimoSail account and all its data at any time, from inside the app, from the web version, or by emailing us. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.

01In the app (quickest)

  1. Open NimoSail and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Choose whether to keep a copy of your sails on your phone, type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account and its data are removed from our servers straight away.

02Without the app

On the web: open the web version of NimoSail, sign in with the same email and password, and follow the same steps (Settings → Account → Delete account).

Open NimoSail on the web

By email: if you can't sign in, email sailapp141@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll check the request comes from that address, delete your account within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

03What gets deleted

Your account
Your email address, password and sign-in.
Your profile
Name, username, photos, contact details and bio.
Your sails
GPS tracks, notes, weather, photos and all other details, plus sails you sent to friends that they haven't added yet.
Boats, crew and plans
Your Fleet, crew records and Noticeboard plans.
Friends
Your friendships and friend requests.
Messages to us
Anything you sent through Contact us.
Error reports
Technical reports the app sent when something went wrong.

04What is kept

  • Nothing on our servers. We don't keep any of your data after your account is deleted.
  • Sails a friend already added to their own log belong to that friend, so they stay in their account.
  • Copies on your phone stay there if you chose to keep them, until you delete them or use Settings → Reset App Data.
