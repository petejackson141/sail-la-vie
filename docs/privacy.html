Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026 · Testing version
Privacy PolicyTerms of Use
IN SHORT
  • We only collect what’s needed to log and share your sails.
  • Your location is used only while you record a sail.
  • Your sails are private unless you choose Friends.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time.
01

Who we are

Sail la Vie is a sailing logbook app made by Pete Jackson, an independent developer based in Israel. In this policy, “we” means Pete Jackson, and “you” means anyone using the app.

If you have a question about your data, email sailapp141@gmail.com.

02

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. Most of it you type in or choose yourself.

Your account
Your email address and password. Passwords are stored encrypted by our database provider and we can never see them.
Your profile
Name, username, role, licence, phone, email, social link, bio, profile photo and cover photo. All of these are optional except your username if you use Friends.
Your sails
GPS track, start and end times, distance and speeds, place, weather, sea state, notes, photos, and the boat and crew you choose.
Boats, crew and plans
Boats in your Fleet, crew records you add, and Noticeboard plans.
Friends
Friend requests you send, receive and accept.
Messages to us
What you write in Contact us, any screenshot you attach, and (if you leave the box ticked) your app version and device type.

We don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don’t collect your contacts.

03

Your location

The app uses your location only while you are recording a sail, to draw your track and measure distance and speed.

On Android you’re asked to allow location “all the time”. This is only so recording keeps going with the screen off during a sail. When no sail is being recorded, the app does not collect your location.

When you cast off, and when your weather report is made, the app sends the position of your sail (latitude and longitude only, nothing about you) to Open-Meteo to get the weather.

04

How we use your data

  • To run the app: save your sails, keep your devices in sync and back up your photos.
  • To show your shared sails and plans to the friends you choose.
  • To reply to your messages and fix problems you report.
  • To keep the app secure and prevent misuse.

We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with anyone except the services listed below, which help run the app.

05

Who can see what

Only you
Everything you log, unless you choose to share it. Sails and plans are private by default.
Your friends
Sails and Noticeboard plans you mark Friends, plus your profile details, and only once you’ve both accepted.
Everyone using the app
Your username, display name and profile photo, so other sailors can find you and send friend requests.
Your crew records
Only you. Crew are private notes and are never shared.
06

Services we use

These companies process data for us to make the app work. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase stores your account, sails, photos and messages on servers in Tokyo, Japan.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of the app and this page.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather for your sail’s position.
  • OpenStreetMap, CARTO and OpenSeaMap provide map images. Like any website, they see your IP address and the map area being shown.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs deliver the app’s fonts and code libraries and see your IP address.
07

Crew and other people

When you add crew or mention people in notes and photos, you’re sharing information about them. Please only add people who are happy to be included. Crew records stay private to you.

08

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete a sail, photo or plan, it’s removed from our servers. When you delete your account, everything linked to it is removed, including messages you’ve sent us. Messages you send us are kept for up to 12 months.

Data saved only on your phone (without an account) stays on your phone until you delete it or use Reset App Data.

09

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data at any time in the app.
  • Download a copy with Settings → Backup to File.
  • Delete anything you’ve logged at any time, or delete your whole account in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and all its data from our servers straight away, and you can choose whether to keep a copy on your phone. If you can’t use the app, email us and we’ll delete it for you within 30 days.
  • Stop location at any time in your phone’s settings. Recording won’t work without it.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under data-protection law. To use any of them, email sailapp141@gmail.com.

10

Keeping it safe

All data travels over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each person read their own data, plus what friends have chosen to share with them. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

11

Children

Sail la Vie is not intended for children under 16. We don’t knowingly collect data from them.

12

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we’ll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
